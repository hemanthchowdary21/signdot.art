Questions?
We’ve got you.

A few quick answers before you get started.

Do you create a signature based on my name?

Yes. The design brief starts with the name you provide and the style you’re drawn to, so the concept is personal to you.

Can I use the design as my legal signature?

A design can be inspiration, but legal or financial signatures should be adopted only if they meet the requirements of the relevant institution and you can reproduce them consistently. We don’t provide legal advice or guarantee acceptance.

Do you make calligraphy as well?

Yes—SignDot’s creative focus includes calligraphy and lettering, alongside personal signature design.

What should I send to get started?

Send the name to be designed, a few words about the vibe you want (for example, minimal, bold, elegant, or playful), and where you intend to use it. Avoid sharing sensitive identity or financial documents.
